## The Idea

Help companies build and run their own sovereign AI models in-house, so no big provider controls their data, decisions, or behavior.

**"Your AI. Your data. Your control."**

## The Problem

1.7M mid-market businesses ($1M-$50M revenue) need AI but have no one to serve them. They are:
- Too big for cookie-cutter SaaS
- Too small for enterprise consulting (McKinsey won't touch them under $500K)
- Ignored by OpenAI/Anthropic who focus on Fortune 500 and consumers

The middle is wide open. These companies have 6-figure AI budgets but zero internal expertise. They want AI but they don't want to hand their data to OpenAI.

## The Offering

We help your company set up a local LLM on your own hardware, fine-tuned on your data. You own it. You control it. No provider can revoke access, raise prices, or see your data.

MVP: Help one company set up a local LLM. Charge $5-10K for the setup. Use open-source models + expertise. Repeat, productize the deployment process.

## Why Now

- Open-source models (Llama, Mistral, Qwen) are good enough for most business tasks
- Hardware is affordable (a Mac Mini cluster or a single GPU server can serve a company)
- Companies are increasingly wary of sending proprietary data to OpenAI
- The mid-market has budget but no expertise: perfect consulting gap

## The Ultimate Vision: Enterprise AI Software Migration

Enterprise LLM starts with "set up your own AI." The ultimate vision is broader:

**"We will migrate any software to custom AI software for your company."**

Every company runs on a stack of generic SaaS tools. We replace them with custom AI-powered software built specifically for that company. The LLM is the brain. The custom software is the body.

This is the convergence point of all three ventures:
- **Aether** provides the agent runtime
- **Enterprise LLM** provides the AI brain (local, sovereign, fine-tuned on company data)
- **CRM As A Service** provides the methodology (observe, learn, spec, build)

## Target Customer

Mid-market companies ($1M-$50M revenue) with:
- 6-figure AI budgets ($100K+ token spend potential)
- Zero internal AI expertise
- Proprietary data they don't want to share with OpenAI
- Need for customization that generic SaaS can't provide

Show cost savings, time savings, or better coverage = instant buy.

## Business Model

- **Phase 1:** Consulting. $5-10K per setup. Help companies deploy local LLMs.
- **Phase 2:** Productize. Standardized deployment packages, repeatable process.
- **Phase 3:** Platform. Self-service deployment + ongoing maintenance retainer.

## Connection to Other Ventures

- **Aether:** The agent runtime. Enterprise LLM is what runs ON Aether for companies.
- **CRM As A Service:** The methodology for replacing SaaS. Enterprise LLM provides the AI brain that powers the custom software.
- **Shared philosophy:** "One LLM per entity" extends from companies to individuals. Aether (personal) + Enterprise LLM (company) = same idea at different scales.
