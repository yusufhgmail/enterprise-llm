# Project Context

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

## Objective

The objective of this project is to create a company that becomes the canvas for achieving the distributed agents mission, profitably.

The company should help move the world toward owned, local, sovereign, distributed agents. Each person and company should be able to own its agentic operating layer: the memory, context, tools, permissions, policies, workflows, identity, logs, and model-routing harness that lets AI act in its interest without becoming dependent on a single frontier LLM provider that can change terms, surveil, throttle, censor, or shut them off.

## Mission Context

Aether's mission document describes the danger we are responding to and the world we want to avoid: centralized control of the agent layer by a handful of frontier AI companies. The concern is not only who owns the model. It is who owns the context, memory, tools, policies, and behavioral defaults that increasingly shape how people and organizations think and act.

It also describes the world we want: many independent agents and agentic operating layers, owned by their users or organizations, aligned with those owners, able to act autonomously, and able to cooperate through a distributed network.

The current `distributedAgentsWorld` project is not identical to Aether. It is the company-formation project for achieving the broader mission articulated in Aether's mission document.

## Relationship To Aether

Aether is one of the projects that aims to take us closer to the world described above. It may become part of the company in the future, but that has not been decided yet.

Current ownership and incorporation status:

- Aether is not yet assigned a final ownership structure.
- Aether is meant to be folded into this company in the future if that proves to be the right structure.
- The exact mechanism is undecided: acquisition, contribution, internal productization, open-source project plus company services, or another structure.
- Until decided, Aether should be treated as related mission infrastructure and context, not automatically as the company itself.

## Current Work Sequence

The intended sequence for this project is:

1. Document the company/Aether/project relationship and mission context.
2. Create a website that promotes what the company does and the mission it stands for.
3. Decide on a roadmap for how the company will achieve the mission.
4. Start executing the first step of that roadmap.
5. The first executable step must include deciding exactly what realistic, profitable goal the company can aim for now.
6. Begin building that first profitable thing.

## Strategic Constraint

The mission is broad and civilizational, but the company must become real through a concrete, profitable wedge.

The immediate strategic question is therefore:

> What is the smallest realistic product or service we can build now that creates revenue while moving customers toward owned, sovereign AI layers that can evolve into distributed agents?

The current candidate for that first wedge is documented in [Master Plan Step 1: Company-Owned AI Layer](master-plan-step-1.md).
