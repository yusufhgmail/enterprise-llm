# NomosLayer alternative expansion roadmap

_Merged into Enterprise LLM on 2026-09-04 from the archived private NomosLayer repository._

## Why this work is retained

NomosLayer contains a broader strategic thesis than the current Works Like Us offer. The brand and separate project are retired, but two ideas remain important:

1. **Public AI chat quietly becomes part of company management.** Employees ask models how to think, decide, prioritize, write, sell, support customers and interpret company strategy. When that happens through a public product, leadership does not control the context, defaults, policies or accumulated operating layer shaping those answers. The model provider gains material influence over everyday company behavior.
2. **A company-owned AI layer gives leadership a new way to steer the company.** The layer can carry company strategy, standards, policies, knowledge and best practices into thousands of small decisions. A leadership change to strategy or policy can change the guidance employees receive throughout the company.

## How it relates to Works Like Us

Works Like Us currently has a deliberately narrower starting point: identify proprietary company work, fine-tune an open model on approved examples, deploy it in infrastructure the company controls and improve it through feedback.

The NomosLayer roadmap shows one way that offer could expand. The trained company model becomes one part of a company-owned operating layer containing memory, context, permissions, policies, tools, workflows, logs, evaluations and model routing. Models can improve or be replaced without giving up the company layer built around them.

This is an alternative expansion roadmap, not the current offer and not evidence that these capabilities have been built or sold.
