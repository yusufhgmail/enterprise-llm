# Domain Search

_Date: 2026-06-01_

## Goal

Find domain names for the company where both the `.com` and `.ai` are available to buy, and the combined cost is under $600.

The name should fit the long-term mission of owned, sovereign, distributed agents, but should mainly support the immediate lead-generation website for the first commercial wedge: private/company-owned LLMs for companies.

## Criteria

- Both `.com` and `.ai` available.
- Combined purchase price under $600.
- Credible in a sales email to a mid-sized company.
- Clear enough that a buyer can infer the category.
- Broad enough that the company can grow beyond "LLMs" into company-owned agents.
- Not tied to Aether, because Aether's ownership and role are not decided yet.

## Sources Checked

- Vercel domain availability and pricing tool.
- Instant Domain Search spot checks for finalists.
- Prior Google Sheet: "My Domains" / "Domain ideas".

The Google Sheet mostly contained older founder/foundry naming research. It confirms `foundryverse.com` and `foundryverse.ai` were previously purchased, but that name does not fit the current private/company-owned LLM wedge strongly enough to recommend as the main domain.

## Esoteric Naming Addendum

After the initial literal search, Yusuf asked for a more esoteric name: something closer to `sovereign.ai` or `Tesla` in spirit. The name should invoke the feeling of the mission without stating the category directly.

The desired feeling:

- Sovereignty without sounding political.
- Owned intelligence without saying "owned AI" literally.
- Local authority, memory, agency, guardianship, and civilization.
- Serious enough for B2B buyers.
- Symbolic enough to become a durable company brand.

The best naming territory found was Latin/Greek institutional language:

- `Civitas` - citizenship, commonwealth, the body of citizens, civic order.
- `Nomos` - law, custom, ordering principle.
- `Custos` - guardian, keeper, protector.
- `Aegis` - shield, protection, sponsorship.
- `Radix` - root, origin, foundation.

Most pure one-word names were unavailable in `.com` and `.ai`, including `sovereign`, `archon`, `nomos`, `polis`, `arete`, `logos`, `civium`, `custos`, `sentinel`, and `locus`.

### Esoteric Recommendation

If choosing an esoteric name, buy:

1. `civitaslayer.com`
2. `civitaslayer.ai`

Why:

`CivitasLayer` gives the company a deeper frame without becoming vague. It suggests a civic/institutional layer of intelligence: the part of the company where norms, strategy, memory, judgment, and representation live. That maps unusually well to the core insight in the master plan: LLMs are becoming the behavioral layer of the company, and that layer should be owned by the company rather than by generic frontier model providers.

It is also broad enough for the long-term mission. A civitas is not just a company; it is a body of members with its own agency and order. That makes the name compatible with sovereign agents, distributed cooperation, and institutional autonomy.

Suggested positioning:

> CivitasLayer builds company-owned AI infrastructure: private LLMs and agents that carry your strategy, protect your data, and help your organization act as one.

### Runner-Up

Buy:

1. `nomoslayer.com`
2. `nomoslayer.ai`

Why:

`NomosLayer` may be the most intellectually precise name. `Nomos` means law, custom, or ordering principle. The name suggests the AI layer that carries a company's way of operating. It is excellent for the thesis that AI chat systems shape employee behavior.

Downside: it is more obscure and may need pronunciation/explanation. `CivitasLayer` is easier to sell.

### Practical Buying Recommendation

If budget allows, buy both pairs:

- `civitaslayer.com` / `civitaslayer.ai`
- `nomoslayer.com` / `nomoslayer.ai`

Combined cost should be about $342.50 based on the registrar prices checked, under the $600 budget.

Use `civitaslayer.ai` or `civitaslayer.com` as the main brand. Hold `nomoslayer` as a possible product, essay series, protocol name, or future redirect.
